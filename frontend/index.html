Upload your smile photos

Front smile is all you need to run analysis. Side profiles are optional and can improve alignment and gum visibility accuracy.

Photo guidance

  • Stand in natural light or soft indoor lighting - avoid harsh flash glare.
  • Show your teeth clearly; lips gently parted or smiling wide.
  • Hold the camera at mouth level, straight on (not from above or below).
  • Use a clean background and remove lipstick if it covers the gumline.
  • Optional: left and right profiles help assess recession and arch form.
